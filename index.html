Privacy Policy — WaveReel

Package ID: com.wavereel.app • Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • Contact: wavereelapp@yahoo.com

1. Overview & On-Device Privacy

WaveReel is a hands-free navigation utility for short-form video feeds (TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts). All screen inspection, content filtering, and gesture execution occur 100% locally on your device. WaveReel does not use the microphone, does not record audio or video, does not take screenshots for external upload, and does not collect, sell, or transmit your personal data or screen content to any developer servers.

2. Use of Android AccessibilityService API

WaveReel requests the Android AccessibilityService permission solely after showing a prominent in-app disclosure and receiving your explicit consent. The service (WaveReel Hands-Free) is used exclusively to:

  • Perform user-configured navigation gestures (next/previous swipe, pause/play, native bookmark save) triggered by the Wet-Hands proximity wave, headset media buttons, or optional timer;
  • Detect video completion and skip user-selected feed items (ads, photo posts, live streams, polls, suggested cards) strictly inside TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

WaveReel does not read notifications outside supported apps, does not monitor passwords or sensitive forms, and does not retain any accessibility tree data after processing a frame in memory.

3. Sensors, Overlay, and Device Permissions

  • Proximity Sensor: Used locally in memory while a supported feed is open to recognize Wet-Hands air-wave gestures.
  • Display Over Other Apps (Overlay): Used strictly to show the compact movable WaveReel floating status indicator during active sessions.
  • Headset Media Controls: Uses a temporary OS MediaSession only when a supported video feed is in the foreground so wired/Bluetooth headset taps trigger navigation instead of background music apps.

4. Network Access, Subscriptions, and Google Play Services

WaveReel includes no third-party advertising SDKs or behavioral analytics trackers. Network access is used solely by official app store system services (such as Google Play Billing and Google Play Services Block Store) to:

  • Display localized pricing and process PRO subscriptions/purchases (wavereel_pro_monthly, wavereel_pro_yearly, wavereel_pro_lifetime);
  • Verify purchase status and store an encrypted/signed 14-day trial and entitlement integrity token via Google Play Block Store so entitlements remain intact across reinstalls on the same device.

5. Data Retention & User Control

Your preferences and HUD coordinates are stored locally on your device. You can reset all app preferences at any time via Android System Settings → Apps → WaveReel → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. Active Google Play subscriptions can be managed or canceled anytime in Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions.

6. Children's Privacy

WaveReel is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or data privacy in WaveReel, please contact: wavereelapp@yahoo.com.

Политика конфиденциальности — WaveReel

Идентификатор приложения: com.wavereel.app • Поддержка: wavereelapp@yahoo.com

Вся обработка содержимого экрана и выполнение жестов через AccessibilityService в приложении WaveReel выполняются 100% локально на вашем устройстве: приложение не использует микрофон, не записывает экран, не собирает и не передаёт содержимое экрана или личные данные на внешние серверы разработчика.

Разрешение AccessibilityService применяется только после вашего явного согласия в окне уведомления исключительно для выбранных вами жестов (взмах «Мокрые руки», кнопки гарнитуры, опциональный автоскролл и пропуск отмеченных типов карточек) в TikTok, Instagram Reels и YouTube Shorts.

Сетевой доступ используется исключительно системными сервисами магазина приложений (Google Play Billing и Google Play Block Store) для обработки покупок и подписки PRO, а также проверки и сохранения целостности 14-дневного пробного периода и статуса PRO. Приложение не содержит сторонних рекламных или аналитических трекеров и не предназначено для детей младше 13 лет.
